# The World Today — 19 November 2026

Front page of the day's world news, as a real broadsheet: lead story the
first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, with the Gulf war
widening, Democrats taking the U.S. Senate, and Grand Theft Auto VI
launching after a 13-year wait. Four realistic press-style photographs,
Times-style serif type, photo captions and two-column body copy.

`python3 compose.py` rebuilds `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) from
the photos in `assets/`; `main.py` copies it byte-for-byte to
`/output/entry.png` (no network, deterministic).
